About Knitfit

A small independent site about an item everyone owns and nobody thinks about.

Socks are the only garment most people buy without reading the label, wash wrongly, and then blame for wearing out.

Why this exists

There is almost no plain writing about socks. There is product copy, and there are forum arguments, and between them sits an ordinary question: why does a drawer full of socks go baggy in a year?

The answer turned out to be the wash rather than the sock, and that was enough to start a site about.

What is here

Two guides and three tools. The Sock Fibre Guide explains the percentages on the packet and what each one is doing. The Washing & Care Guide covers the five habits that decide how long a pair lasts.

The tools answer one question each, in the browser, with nothing stored and no sign-up.

How we stay independent

No brand, mill or retailer can pay to be mentioned here. There are no shopping or affiliate links anywhere on the site, and nothing is sent to us for review. Advertising is served by a third party and has no say in what we publish.

We also do not sell socks, which is why this site is happy to tell you to buy fewer and wash them cooler.

Where we stop

Nothing here is medical advice. The blister tool is about friction, moisture and time, not treatment, and a blister that is painful, discoloured or will not heal should be seen by a pharmacist or a doctor. The Disclaimer sets this out in full.

Accuracy

Lifespan figures are ranges, because shoe fit and gait affect wear more than fibre does. Where we are estimating we say so in the tool itself, and we would rather be useful and honest about the error bars than precise and wrong.

Corrections

Write to hello@knitfit.com and a person will reply within two business days. Corrections go to the front of the queue, and the Editorial Policy explains how we research and review.
